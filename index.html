🌿 HabitsTrack full wins, small wins, and bare-minimum wins⌄
  • 🕐Habits can be grouped by morning, afternoon, or evening
  • ✓Full version — you did your normal version
  • ○Small version — a smaller valid version, counts just the same
  • ·Bare-minimum version — the smallest thing that still counts
  • —“Not today” or Skip is neutral data, not a failure
  • ↩Returned — coming back after a missed day. That’s the whole goal

💛 CircleRemember who matters, without the pressure⌄

  • 💬Contact ideas — a low-effort way to reach out
  • 🕊️Next soft check-in — a gentle nudge, not a deadline
  • 📝Person notes — small things worth remembering
This is not a guilt list. It is a care reminder.
