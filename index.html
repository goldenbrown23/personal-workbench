🧠 How this works

The tracker is intentionally designed not to punish imperfect days.
✓ Done
You did your normal version.
○ Counted
You used a smaller valid version. It counts exactly the same toward engagement.
— Not today
Neutral data. Nothing needs fixing tonight.
↩ Returned
The first time you engage again after a missed day. This is the behavior we want to strengthen.
After a miss:
Do not compensate. Do not restart the week. Do not redesign the system. Just make contact with the habit again.
